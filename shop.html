What the notice does

California law makes a 30 day written notice the precondition to a CLRA damages claim under section 1782. Most merchants would rather refund a $40 renewal and pay the postage than answer one. Whichever way it goes, the notice exists and the clock runs.

The aggressive part, because it is true

Where the court goes past the cure, section 1780(e) puts the consumer's attorney fees on the merchant if it prevails, restated twice, pausing extended and citation, section 17602 adds a civil penalty per violation, and a willful showing needs no proof of intent. None of that is quoted to you as a promised number. The notice states the exposure, the merchant prices its own risk, and the file settles on their number or files.
